Almost-free SEO — a practical guide for any business

Works with or without OpenClaw. This is how small businesses get to page 1 without paying £100/month for Semrush.

1️⃣

Find quick wins in Search Console (free)

Connect Google Search Console to your site (free). Find every keyword you already rank for in positions 8–20 — these are your fastest wins. You're nearly on page 1. Write better, longer content targeting each one and you'll jump within weeks.

2️⃣

Keyword research — without paying for tools

Without OpenClaw: Google Keyword Planner (free), Google autocomplete, and Answer The Public (3 free searches/day) give you real data. With OpenClaw: Ask it to generate 30 long-tail keywords for your niche in 30 seconds — it reasons about search intent, competition and seasonality automatically.

3️⃣

Write content that ranks

Without OpenClaw: Write 1,500+ word posts targeting one keyword, structured with H2s and H3s, answering the questions people actually search for. With OpenClaw: Ask it to write the full post — it includes headers, internal link suggestions, meta description, and FAQs automatically. What took 4 hours takes 3 minutes.

4️⃣

Fix on-page SEO across your whole site

Without OpenClaw: Use Screaming Frog (free up to 500 pages) to find missing meta descriptions, duplicate titles, and broken links. Fix manually. With OpenClaw: Paste any page in and ask it to audit and rewrite your title tag, meta description, headers and keyword usage on the spot.

5️⃣

Build local SEO pages that dominate

For local businesses this is the single biggest opportunity. Ask OpenClaw to write a location-specific landing page for every town or city you serve. Each one targets a local keyword (e.g. "plumber in Bristol"). Agencies charge £300–500 per page. OpenClaw does it in 2 minutes.

6️⃣

Schema markup — agencies charge £500 for this

Ask OpenClaw to generate LocalBusiness, FAQ, Product or Review schema for any page. Paste the code into your site and rich results appear in Google search — star ratings, FAQ dropdowns, business hours. Most businesses have never done this.

7️⃣

Backlinks — the free way

Ask OpenClaw to find relevant industry blogs, directories and journalist query sites (like HARO) to target. It can draft your outreach emails, write your guest post pitches, and identify broken link opportunities on competitor sites — all the tactics agencies bill £1,000+/month for.

8️⃣

Put it on autopilot

Set OpenClaw to publish one new SEO article every week automatically — keyword researched, written, formatted and posted to your CMS while you sleep. One year of consistent publishing and your organic traffic looks completely different. This is what separates businesses that grow from ones that stay invisible.
